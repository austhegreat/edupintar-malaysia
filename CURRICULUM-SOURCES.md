# Curriculum review notes

This file records the references used when curating chapter sequences. The notes and interactive activities in this app are original learning aids; teachers should check them against the current KPM DSKP and the textbook adopted by their school before using them as a formal assessment source.

## Secondary school — KSSM Mathematics, Forms 1–3

- The 13/13/9 chapter names and order for Forms 1/2/3 were checked against the lower-secondary KSSM Mathematics chapter outline published by Afterkelas, which links to Bahagian Pembangunan Kurikulum (BPK) Mathematics documents: <https://www.afterkelas.com/tuition/lower-secondary/maths>
- The Ministry of Education school resource page lists the KSSM Form 1 Mathematics DSKP: <https://sites.google.com/moe-dl.edu.my/unitkurikulumsmkusj13/dskp/bidang-sains-matematik>
- The Form 1 Mathematics DSKP can also be read in the reproduced document here: <https://asiemodel.net/wp-content/uploads/2022/08/DSKP-Mathematics-Form-1.pdf>

The app currently includes original BM notes with two or three subtopics per chapter for Form 1–3 Mathematics. English DLP translations and reviewed quizzes are still to be prepared. Curriculum coverage and substantive notes for the remaining secondary subjects and Forms 4–5 Mathematics are still outstanding. Draft chapters remain unavailable as learning activities until they have substantive notes.

## Primary school — KSSR Semakan 2017

- KPM Circular 6/2024 introduced the KSSR Semakan 2017 Edition 3 curriculum-alignment documents for Mathematics and Science in Tahap I. KPM describes the alignment documents as support material to use with the DSKP and MOBIM, not as replacements for the full DSKP: <https://www.moe.gov.my/storage/files/shares/pekeliling_dan_garis_panduan/surat_siaran/bahagian-pengurusan-sekolah-harian/Surat%20Siaran%20KPM%20Bil.%206%202024%20Pelaksanaan%20Dokumen%20Penjajaran%20Kurikulum%20Standard%20Sekolah%20Rendah%20Edisi%203%20Sains%20Dan%20Math%20Tahap%201.pdf>
- KPM Circular 1/2025 introduced Edition 3 alignment documents for Bahasa Melayu and Bahasa Inggeris in Tahap I, likewise as support used alongside DSKP, MOBIM and the English Scheme of Work: <https://www.moe.gov.my/storage/files/shares/pekeliling_dan_garis_panduan/surat_siaran/bahagian-pengurusan-sekolah-harian/Surat%20Siaran%20Bil.1%20Pelaksanaan%20Dokumen%20Penjajaran%20Kurikulum%20BM%20Dan%20BI.pdf>
- The app keeps the current KSSR Semakan grade map and displays Sejarah for Tahun 4–6. KPM has announced that under the forthcoming Kurikulum Persekolahan 2027, primary Sejarah will be offered in Tahun 5 and 6; update the grade map and topic sequence when that curriculum takes effect: <https://www.moe.gov.my/storage/files/shares/Kenyataan%20Media/KM2026/KM%20KPM%20Berhubung%20Kurikulum%20Baharu%20Mata%20Pelajaran%20Sejarah%20Sekolah%20Rendah%20Hanya%20Ditawarkan%20Kepada%20Murid%20Tahun%205%20Dan%20Tahun%206.pdf>

This release adds a structured set of original learning notes, subtopics, short practice questions and lightweight interactive activities for Mathematics, Science, Bahasa Melayu and English in Tahun 1–6, plus Sejarah in Tahun 4–6. The topic sequence is a curated learning aid and does not claim complete DSKP coverage. Teachers should check lesson details against the current DSKP and the school's adopted textbook, particularly as the 2027 curriculum transition approaches.
